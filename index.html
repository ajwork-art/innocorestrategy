TrustNet™ Platform

Runtime AI Governance,
Built for Production

TrustNet™ is Innocore's patent-pending AI governance platform that enforces policies, generates trust evidence, and keeps humans in authority — in real-time, at every AI decision point.

Patent Pending

Policy-Driven Enforcement

TrustNet™ translates your governance policies into machine-executable rules that operate in real time — intercepting, evaluating, and enforcing AI outputs before they reach end users or downstream systems. No more governance that lives only in documents.

Trust Evidence Pack

Every AI decision produces a cryptographically linked audit trail — the Trust Evidence Pack. Designed for regulatory examiners, board risk committees, and internal audit functions, TrustNet™ makes your AI's reasoning visible, reviewable, and defensible.

Responsible AI by Design

Responsible AI is not a retrofit. TrustNet™ embeds fairness monitoring, bias detection, and human override pathways directly into the AI workflow architecture — ensuring that governance is operational from day one, not bolted on after a regulatory finding.

Innocore's Proprietary Framework

Human Value Governance™

HVG™ is the governance philosophy at the core of everything Innocore builds. It defines how enterprises should structure AI accountability so that humans remain in authority — and can prove it.

01

Governance in Motion

Governance must be active, not archival. HVG™ requires that AI policies be enforced continuously at the point of decision — not audited retroactively after harm has occurred. Governance in Motion means the guardrails are always on.

Always-On Enforcement
02

Evidence by Default

Every AI action should produce verifiable evidence as a natural byproduct — not as an add-on compliance exercise. HVG™ treats the audit trail as a first-class output, enabling organizations to demonstrate responsible AI behavior on demand, to any stakeholder.

Audit-Ready by Design
03

Human Authority Preserved

AI systems must be designed with explicit human override pathways and decision escalation protocols. HVG™ ensures that no AI system can remove human judgment from high-stakes decisions — and that the chain of authority is documented and enforceable.

Human-in-the-Loop
